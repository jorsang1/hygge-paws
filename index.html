Our Philosophy

A calm alternative
to big daycare

We welcome only 3–5 dogs per day to create a peaceful routine with quality care.

Ideal for sensitive, senior, or calm dogs that thrive in low-stress spaces.

Your dog does not have to spend the day alone at home. At Hygge Paws, experienced and loving care means safe, happy hours with walks, rest, and calm companionship while you work.

Not our style

  • Mass daycare with too many dogs
  • Loud, stressful environment
  • Chaotic routines
  • Aversive methods

What dogs get at Hygge Paws

  • Small groups (max 3–5 dogs)
  • Long outdoor time and nature walks
  • Rest, calm cuddles, and indoor comfort
  • Clear communication and daily photos
A cozy and safe environment where your dog can relax, play and enjoy the day while you're at work.

Admission

Important admission rules

To protect the calm group dynamic, we only accept dogs that fit our environment.

No aggressive dogs
No severe anxiety cases without prior experience support
Vaccinations must be up to date
No conflict-prone males in group settings
Dogs must be able to stay indoors calmly
Meet & greet is mandatory before the first day

Clear expectations from the start help every dog enjoy a safe, relaxed day.
