Important admission rules

To protect the calm group dynamic, we only accept dogs that fit our environment.

  • No aggressive dogs
  • No severe anxiety cases without prior experience support
  • Vaccinations must be up to date
  • No conflict-prone males in group settings
  • Dogs must be able to stay indoors calmly

Clear expectations from the start help every dog enjoy a safe, relaxed day.
