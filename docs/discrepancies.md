## Detailed Findings (Post-Update)

### 1. Group Size ❌
**Current Discrepancy:**
- **Website:** "We welcome only 3–5 dogs per day"
- **Brochures:** Show maximum 3–4 dogs

**Issue:** The website's range is broader (up to 5) than what brochures advertise (max 4).

**Decision from previous review:** Max 3 dogs, with 4 as legal limit. Website should be clarified.

**Action needed:** Update website to align with brochure (max 3-4 dogs, not 3-5).

---

### 2. Arrival Time Details ✅ (Flexible)
- **Website standard:** 09:00–10:00 arrival
- **Website flexibility note:** "Entry between 08:00 and 09:00 can be arranged if needed"
- **Brochures:** 08:30–09:30 arrival window

**Status:** Website offers flexibility; brochures show standard window. Both mention arrangements available.

---

### 3. Contact Information ✅
- Email: `info@hyggepaws.com` — **Aligned** across all materials
- Phone: Not displayed on website (decision made to keep it on brochure only)
- Instagram: Not displayed on website (decision made)

---

### 4. Opening Hours & Flexibility ✅
- **Standard:** 09:00–16:00
- **Flexibility:** Website explicitly states options for 08:00-09:00 drop-off and 16:00-17:00 pick-up
- **Brochure note:** "Early drop-off available on demand"

**Status:** Aligned through flexibility disclaimer.

---

### 5. Meet & Greet ✅
- **Website:** "Meet & greet is mandatory before the first day" — Listed in admission rules
- **Brochures:** Clearly shown as required step 1

**Status:** Aligned. Both emphasize it's mandatory.

**Action needed:** Consider adding these operational details to the website's admission rules or a FAQ section.
